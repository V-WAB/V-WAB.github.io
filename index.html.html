The collectors are already registered. The AMA ran a free, compulsory registration of borla taxi operators, with an official sticker — and Ga East, one of our two pilot assemblies, formalised over 150 collectors through its own Borla Taxi and Tricycle Association. We are not building a network. We are switching one on.

Rates here are the rates in the costing model: GH¢0.70 per kg for sorted recyclables (GH¢700 per tonne) and GH¢0.12 per kg for organics, each split between the household and the collector. *233# is illustrative — the live shortcode would be assigned by the NCA.
