Privacy

What I hold, and what I do not.

This is a short policy, because Banini Butter is a small operation and I ask for very little.

Last updated 17 September 2026

What I ask you for

If you join the waitlist, I take your email address and nothing else.

If you reserve a jar, I take your name, your email address and your phone number, so that I can write to you and reach you about your jars. You may add your town or city and a note if you want to, and both are optional. I also keep which blends, skin types and sizes you chose.

What I never ask you for

Card details, mobile money details, or anything else to do with payment. Reservations are free, nothing is charged, and there is no checkout on this site that could take a payment even if you wanted to make one.

Why I keep it

To write to you when the first run is whipped, to confirm your reservation and what it comes to, and to arrange getting it to you. That is the whole of it. I do not sell it, rent it, or hand it to anyone else for their marketing.

Where it is kept

  • Supabase holds the waitlist and the reservations in a database.
  • Resend sends the emails, so your address passes through them whenever I write to you.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser collects them from Google as the page loads, which means Google can see your IP address. I send them nothing else about you.

Cookies and tracking

There are none. This site sets no cookies, saves nothing in your browser, and runs no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Nothing here follows you anywhere else.

How long I keep it

For as long as it is doing something useful, and no longer. Ask me to remove you and I remove you.

What you can ask me to do

Write to me and I will tell you what I hold about you, correct anything that is wrong, take you off the waitlist, or delete you from my records altogether. Every email I send also carries an unsubscribe link.

Getting in touch

Write to hello@baninibutter.com about anything on this page, or about anything at all.
